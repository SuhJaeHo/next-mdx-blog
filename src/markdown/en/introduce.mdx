To determine whether development suited me, I took a leave of absence and completed a Java-based web development course. After graduating, I completed Vanilla Coding's frontend-focused full-stack bootcamp and later joined Wemade as a frontend engineer.

The experience of learning firsthand and building services became the foundation of how I approach development: quickly understanding new technologies and code structures, then turning them into working features.

## Performance Optimization and Stable Operation of a Real-Time Trading Service

At Wemade, I worked on the web frontend of an order-book-based game-token exchange. In a small team, I gained experience from initial design through launch and operations. Even as ownership changed, I quickly understood the existing architecture and trading logic and continued operating the service with the remaining team.

I was particularly responsible for improving the performance of screens receiving continuous, high-volume data such as live prices and order books. I buffered WebSocket data and applied it at regular intervals to reduce unnecessary rendering, while using React's concurrency features to separate update priorities. As a result, the interface remained responsive in tests simulating large-scale traffic.

This experience taught me that frontend engineering is not simply about implementing screens, but about considering networks, data flow, and browser rendering together to create the stability users actually experience.

## Practical Architecture for the Situation at Hand

At NEXUS, I developed products with very different characteristics: a wallet, an exchange, a public-sale platform, and a staking service. I prioritize architecture appropriate to the schedule, team, and operating environment—not simply the newest or most elaborate solution.

For a public-sale service, I delivered project setup, wallet connection, KYC, RPC configuration, and smart-contract calls in one week. When public RPC rate limits delayed data retrieval during operations, I identified the cause and migrated to a JWT-authenticated private RPC. The service completed a public sale worth approximately KRW 20 billion without a critical incident.

For a token exchange, I unified duplicated transaction logic behind an adapter and migrated the application from a monorepo into an independent project. I also built generation of DTOs, entities, and repositories from Swagger specifications. These changes preserved all existing behavior while reducing the codebase by approximately 30%.

To me, practical engineering does not mean insisting on a particular technology or design pattern. It means avoiding unnecessary abstraction and excessive complexity while choosing a structure that is easy to change and maintain.

My strengths are quickly understanding unfamiliar systems, dividing problems into solvable units, and delivering a working product within a fixed schedule.
