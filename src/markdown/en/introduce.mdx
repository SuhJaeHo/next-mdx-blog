# Frontend Engineer Improving Service Quality

For about four years, I have worked as a frontend engineer across early service design, launch, operations, performance improvement, and legacy refactoring. I value solving complex problems in the simplest and most practical way possible, and I have focused on improving the stability and quality that users can actually experience. I also believe good results are created by teams, not individuals, and I collaborate with respect and consideration for my colleagues.

## Confirming My Path Through Experience

While majoring in Applied Chemical Engineering at Korea University of Technology and Education, I spent a lot of time thinking about my career path. As I prepared for the civil-service exam and continued my major studies, I reflected on what I truly wanted to do and remembered how much I enjoyed a C-language class during my first year. That experience led me to become interested in software development as a career.

To directly confirm whether development suited me, I took a leave of absence and completed a Java-based web development course. I felt a strong sense of achievement in analyzing problems, solving them, and creating working results, which led me to choose development as my career.

After graduating, I completed a frontend-focused full-stack course at Vanilla Coding Bootcamp and began my career as a frontend developer at Wemade. I still enjoy learning new technologies and thinking about how to create better user experiences and higher service quality as I continue to grow.

## Development With Performance and User Experience in Mind

At Wemade, I worked on the web frontend of an order-book-based game-token exchange. In a small team, I experienced the process from initial design through launch and operations, and even after the team was reduced, I quickly understood the existing architecture and trading logic to keep the service running reliably.

I was responsible for improving the performance of screens where large volumes of data continuously flowed in, such as real-time prices and order books. I buffered real-time data and applied it in batches at regular intervals to reduce unnecessary rendering, and adjusted rendering priorities so the user experience could remain stable even when large amounts of data were coming in.

I think about how to maximize the user experience while implementing the required features, and my goal is not only to complete products within the schedule but also to improve their quality so they can operate reliably after release.

## Goals Ahead

I want to deepen my frontend expertise while broadening my understanding of backend systems and infrastructure, growing into a software engineer who can consider the overall data flow and structure of a service.

Rather than staying within a specific technology or domain, I want to experience diverse services, quickly understand requirements and user problems, and build the ability to propose technical solutions appropriate to each situation. I also want to contribute to my team by balancing schedule and quality, not stopping at simply implementing assigned features but helping the team complete reliable outcomes.

I also want to use AI tools not merely as a code-generation aid, but across the development process, including requirements analysis, repetitive-work automation, testing, and code review, to improve productivity and the quality of deliverables.

In the long term, my goal is to participate in technical decision-making beyond the boundary between frontend and backend, and to grow into a software engineer who builds stable and convenient services that users can be satisfied with.
